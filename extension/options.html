📘 My Kitchen Notes — Save recipes from Facebook, Instagram & TikTok

A “Save recipe” button appears under Facebook, Instagram and TikTok posts that have text, and on a reel or video page. Clicking it opens the post’s “See more”, takes the post’s text (not the comments) and opens your collection with it, ready to import.

On any other website: press the toolbar button (or Alt+Shift+S), or right-click the page → “Save recipe”. It sends the recipe the page publishes for search engines, or the text you selected. Right-click a link → “Import this link” to import that page instead.

One step left: allow the extension on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, so the “Save recipe” button can appear under posts there.
