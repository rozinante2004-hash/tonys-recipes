# Privacy — “My Kitchen Notes — Save recipes from any site” (browser extension)

**In short: the extension reads a post only when you click its button, and sends it nowhere except to your own
My Kitchen Notes collection, in your own browser.**

- **What it reads:** when you click “📘 Save recipe to My Kitchen Notes” under a Facebook, Instagram or TikTok post,
  it opens that post’s “See more” and reads the post’s text and its link. When you use the toolbar button, its shortcut or the right-click menu on any
  page, it reads the recipe the page publishes for search engines, or the text you selected, or the page’s main text.
  “Import this link” sends only the link you right-clicked. Nothing is read before you click.
- **Where it goes:** the extension opens My Kitchen Notes in a new tab with that text in the part of the web
  address after “#”. That part of an address is never sent to any server; the app reads it in your browser and
  shows it to you as a recipe to import. You decide whether to keep it.
- **On My Kitchen Notes' own pages:** it marks the page so the app knows the extension is installed (and stops
  offering it). It reads nothing there. It remembers, in this browser only, whether you are signed in to the family's
  copy of the app, so that recipes go there rather than to My Kitchen Notes; that one setting is all it keeps.
- **What it does not do:** it makes no network requests of its own, keeps no copy of what it read, collects no
  browsing history, contains no analytics or advertising, and does not read any site while you are not clicking.
- **Your accounts:** the extension never sees, stores or sends your Facebook, Instagram or TikTok password or session.
