Privacy Statement

My Kitchen Notes — the web app (including its family, test and beta copies) and the “My Kitchen Notes — Save recipes from any site” browser extension.
Effective date: 6 October 2026 · Version 1.0
In short. We keep your recipes and what is needed to share them with the people you choose. We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use analytics or tracking cookies. Some features send specific content to outside services (for example, text you ask the AI about); this statement lists every one of them. You can download your data and delete your account from within the app at any time.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Tony Schvekher, a private individual in Israel (“we”, “us”, the “Operator”), who is the controller (“owner of the database”) of the personal data described here.

Contact for any privacy matter: tony.schvekher@gmail.com.

This statement is written to meet Israel’s Protection of Privacy Law, 5741‑1981 and its regulations (including Amendment 13), and, for people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and its equivalents.

4. Who else can see your data

  • Your household: everyone in your household sees its recipes, photos, members (names and e-mail addresses), link requests and conversations. Owners and admins can add and remove people; the owner can delete the household.
  • Linked households: the people in a household linked with yours can read your household’s recipes and photos (read-only), and the conversation between the two households. They do not see your members’ e-mail addresses.
  • Households you ask to link with: see your household’s name and identifier, your note and your messages — signed with the household’s name, never a person’s. If you include a phone number, e-mail address or link in a message, they will see it.
  • The Operator can see household summaries (names, identifiers, members’ e-mail addresses, links, usage) and the notes and reports you send, in order to administer the service. The Operator does not read the content of your recipes, photos or chats, except where you send it to us, where you ask us to help, or where required to delete a household or investigate abuse.

5. International transfers

Our providers store and process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where data leaves Israel or the European Economic Area, it is transferred under the safeguards the law provides — for providers in the United States, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated in the provider’s terms — and, for transfers from Israel, under the Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761‑2001.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: until you delete them, delete your household, or delete your account. Deleted content is removed from the live database at once; copies in providers’ backups expire according to their schedules.
  • A household’s summary, its usage counts, and the record that it was deleted (by whom, and when): kept after deletion for administration, accounting and abuse prevention, for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. You may ask us to delete them (section 8).
  • Notes and reports you sent us: until we delete them.
  • The log on your device: 7 days, then deleted automatically. Backups you save are files on your own device; we have no copy.

7. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access to each household is enforced by database rules that check the person’s sign-in and role. Server keys are never placed in the App. No system is perfectly secure: we cannot guarantee that unauthorised access, loss or disclosure will never happen. If a breach is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the competent authority as the law requires. Please keep your sign-in secure, sign out on shared devices, and keep backups of anything important.

8. Your rights

Subject to the conditions of the applicable law, you may:

  • access the data we hold about you, and receive a copy (Settings → Backups saves your collection; for anything else, write to us);
  • have inaccurate data corrected or completed;
  • have data deleted (Settings → Delete my account deletes your account and the households you own alone; or write to us);
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive data you provided in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Under Israeli law you may inspect data about you held in a database (section 13 of the Protection of Privacy Law) and request its correction or deletion (section 14). Write to tony.schvekher@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority, or, in the EEA/UK, to your local data protection authority.

9. Cookies and device storage

The App uses your browser’s local storage, IndexedDB and a service worker to store your settings and an offline copy of your collection, and Google’s sign-in uses its own cookies to keep you signed in. These are strictly necessary for the App to work. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and no third-party trackers.

10. Children

The App is intended for people aged 16 and over. A younger child may use it only as a member of a parent’s or guardian’s household, added by that parent or guardian, who consents to this statement on the child’s behalf and is responsible for the child’s use. If we learn that we hold data of a child under 16 without such consent, we will delete it. Parents may contact us at any time.

11. The browser extension

  • It reads a post only when you click its “Save recipe” button, or the page when you click its toolbar button; it then opens My Kitchen Notes with that text in the part of the web address after “#”, which is not sent to any server.
  • On My Kitchen Notes’ own pages it marks the page so the App knows the extension is installed. It reads nothing there.
  • It makes no network requests of its own, keeps no copy of what it read, collects no browsing history, contains no analytics, and never sees, stores or sends your Facebook, Instagram or TikTok password or session.
  • What happens to the text once it reaches the App is described in the rest of this statement.
