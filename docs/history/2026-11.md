- **v40.02 + Worker v108 — 🔔 chat notifications when the app is closed (20 Nov 2026; on Test).** Tony: "Also, when
  using the chat in "Chat with family members", is there a way to make notifications audible even if the app is not
  running?" — then: "Yes, build it, show the message text, both family and linked kitchen chats. Let's leave the Pushover
  for now."
  - **How it works:** Web Push, the browsers' own (Apple's since iOS 16.4, for the app on the Home Screen). 🔔 Notify me
    of new messages when the app is closed, at the foot of 👪 Chat with family members and of 💬 Chats with linked
    kitchens — one switch per device for both. The tap asks the browser, the device gets an address at its push service,
    the Worker keeps it (D1 `push_subs`). A message sent: the sender's app asks the Worker (`push-notify`), which READS
    THE MESSAGE FROM THE DATABASE WITH THE SENDER'S SIGN-IN — so only a real message of theirs, from the last ten minutes,
    once — and sends it, encrypted for each device, to those who may read it: the household's other members (the one
    person, for a message to one person), checked against the members list at that moment; for a kitchen chat only a
    LINKED kitchen (not one still asking, refused or blocked), and its devices heard from in the last 45 days. Never the
    sender's own devices. The notification: "👪 Dana (to everyone)" / "💬 Dana's Kitchen" and the text (300 characters),
    the phone's own sound; a tap opens that conversation. While the app is on the screen it chimes itself instead (on
    Apple's devices the notification shows anyway: Safari takes the permission away from a site whose push showed nothing).
  - **The key** — VAPID, the Worker's secret `VAPID_JWK`: made by the deploy job from the Cloudflare token it already
    holds (`tools/vapid-key.mjs`, as the usage watch's key), the same on every deploy, piped straight into `wrangler
    secret put`, never printed. (I had told Tony the keys would be kept in GitHub like the other secrets; this way there
    is nothing new to keep at all — it lives in Cloudflare with the Worker's other keys.) A new Cloudflare token would
    make a new key: the apps notice at `push-key` and sign their devices up again.
  - **Checked here:** `tests/worker-cors.mjs` — 33 new checks: the key, signing up (sign-in, confirmed address, a member,
    a real push service, real device keys), each message decrypted by a SEPARATE implementation of RFC 8291 written from
    the RFC with node:crypto (the Worker's uses WebCrypto) — and, outside the repository, by the `http_ece` library; the
    VAPID JWT verified (ES256, the service's origin, under a day); one person only; not someone else's message; not an
    old one; once; someone who left hears nothing and their device is forgotten; 410 forgets; a linked kitchen yes, an
    unlinked one no; 45 days; ten devices a person. Thirteen deliberate breaks of the Worker each fail a check.
  - **Self Tests:** `chat_notifications_switch` (what each kind of device is told; the switch at the foot of the window
    survives a redraw; hidden when the server cannot), `chat_notifications_announced` (famSend announces the id it wrote;
    nobody else in the household → nothing; a kitchen not linked → nothing; never during a Self Test), and
    `chat_notifications_shown_and_tapped` (the real sw.js `push` / `notificationclick` handlers through
    `tests/sw-probe.js`: shown when the app is closed, not when it is on the screen, always on an iPhone; a tap opens the
    app at the conversation or brings it forward); `kitchen_chats` checks the switch in its window. Local: **497 pass, 0
    fail, 7 skipped** in English and Hebrew; knock-out 4/4.
  - **Privacy Statement 1.18** (20 Nov, all 11 pages by hand, re-keyed): what is kept for notifications and why
    (consent, by turning them on), the notification services as recipients (encrypted, they cannot read it), and how long
    (until turned off or signed out, or the service says the address is gone; 45 days for a linked kitchen's messages).
    ⚙️ → What leaves this device: "Your device's notification service". Help: the assistant's 🔔 CHAT NOTIFICATIONS line.
